# symfony_shop

Version: 5.0.4

Cart and catalog for Symfony: price snapshots, variations, schedules, stock reservation, checkout through symfony-payment

## Table of Contents

- [Introduction](#introduction)

`symfony-shop` sells through carts: products with variations, schedules and limited stock, carts that may start before their buyer has an account, and a checkout paid through `symfony-payment`. What happens once a cart is paid — an invoice, a membership, a mail — belongs to the app, through events and per-product-type handlers.
